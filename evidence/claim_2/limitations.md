# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

- The noisy end-to-end instance is quadratic with a first-order signal. The
  exact basis-map argument extends to fixed finite degree, but higher-order
  signal search was not experimentally exercised.
- The result uses ideal D-RUT effective phases; finite-Trotter and hardware
  synthesis errors are not simulated.
- The empirical first-hit distribution is finite and its disjoint success
  uncertainty is reported with Wilson intervals.
- The theorem gives an upper bound, not a lower bound over every algorithm.
