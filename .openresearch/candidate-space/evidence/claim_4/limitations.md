# Claim 4 limitations and deviations

- This verifies the covariance theorem under the appendix's linear,
  equal-isotropic-variance, independent-dataset model. It does not establish
  that a particular bosonic device satisfies that noise model.
- The paper prints only the two marginal block inequalities in Equations (106)
  and (108). The verifier reconstructs the omitted cross-covariance and proves
  the stronger full joint Loewner inequality written in the main-results text.
- The proof assumes all inverses shown by the paper exist. Rank-deficient
  designs would require a separate pseudoinverse statement and are outside
  this contract.
- Numerical random cases and Monte Carlo are diagnostics. They are not used as
  proof of the universal statement; that role is supplied by the algebraic
  factorization and exact rational certificate.
- The historical equal-trace example remains preserved and is reproduced as
  the orthogonal-design equality case. Equality is allowed by “lower or
  equal,” but it is not presented as evidence of strict improvement.
